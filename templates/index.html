Questions

Do I need the wearable to try it?

No. Press “Try the live demo” to explore every screen with simulated data. To track your real posture you need the BioSpine sensor.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes, the whole app is designed for phones and can be installed to your home screen. Bluetooth pairing works in Chrome on Android. iPhone browsers do not support Web Bluetooth, so on iPhone use the demo, review your history, or pair from a Bluetooth-capable browser app.

Can it remind me to take back breaks during meetings?

Yes. On the Back Break page choose how often (every 30 minutes up to 2 hours) and switch on Meeting-friendly mode: no sound, no pop-ups, just a small quiet note in the corner.

Is the AI giving medical advice?

No. The AI Physio gives general ergonomics and stretching tips and can be wrong. It is not a medical device or a diagnosis. See the health disclaimer.

      Neck load by lean angle

      Research (Hansraj, 2014) estimated how much force the neck carries as the head tips forward:

      LeanForce on neckIn kgWhat it means
      0° (straight)10-12 lbs~5 kgRelaxed and healthy
      15° (small lean)27 lbs~12 kgNeck muscles start to tire
      30° (big lean)40 lbs~18 kgHeavy strain if you stay like this
      45°+ (head down)49-60 lbs22-27 kgVery high strain; stiff neck and shoulders likely

      What is RULA?

      A standard workplace check of how arms, neck and back are positioned. The final score tells you how soon to act:

      Level 1 · score 1-2Fine. Just keep moving now and then.
      Level 2 · score 3-4Small changes may help. Keep an eye on it.
      Level 3 · score 5-6Please change your set-up soon.
      Level 4 · score 7Change your set-up as soon as possible.
