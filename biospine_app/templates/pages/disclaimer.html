Health & AI Disclaimer

{{ SITE_NAME }} is a wellness and ergonomics tool. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any disease or injury.

Posture data

Angles, scores and "cervical load" figures are estimates produced by a wearable sensor and general biomechanics models. They can be inaccurate, especially if the sensor is worn incorrectly or not calibrated.

AI Physio and AI diagnosis

The AI features are generated by a large-language model (Google Gemini). They can make mistakes, and they are general wellness suggestions - not medical advice, not a diagnosis, and not a replacement for a doctor or physiotherapist.

See a professional if…

  • you have numbness, tingling or weakness in your arms or hands,
  • pain is severe, getting worse, or started after an injury,
  • you have headaches with vision changes, dizziness, or chest pain,
  • discomfort does not improve after a couple of weeks of better posture and regular breaks.

In an emergency contact your local emergency number immediately. Do not delay seeking care because of anything shown in this app.

Stop any exercise that causes pain and ask a qualified professional before starting a new routine, especially if you have a spine, neck or shoulder condition.
